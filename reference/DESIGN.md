## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an avant-garde digital canvas for a creative technologist. It balances architectural restraint with kinetic engineering precision, merging Swiss typographic rigor, minimal brutalism, and editorial dark luxury. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Architectural & Intentional:** Every division is razor-sharp; hairline spatial dividers replace heavy shadows or container boxes.
- **Hyper-Technical Yet Human:** Code execution meets high-concept digital gallery curation. Monospaced telemetry counters sit beside sweeping, sculptural display typography.
- **Subdued Luxury:** The UI avoids neon oversaturation in favor of deep spectral obsidian surfaces punctuated by precise, bioluminescent emerald and mint accents.
- **Atmosphere:** Evokes the feeling of an elite architectural studio terminal, high-end computational research deck, and an Awwwards Site of the Year portfolio.

### Visual Philosophy
1. **Zero SaaS Fluff:** No pillowy pastel drop shadows, decorative bubbly blobs, or generic white-boxed cards.
2. **Hairline Precision:** Spatial containment relies on single-pixel hairline grid lines (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and monolithic asymmetric gutters.
3. **Typography as Structure:** Scale, weight contrast, and strict tracking define visual hierarchy before color is introduced.

## Colors

The palette leverages pure optical darkness overlaid with cold-cast graphite surfaces, engineered for ultra-high-contrast legibility and OLED fidelity. Luminous accents are applied strictly as surgical focal points (live states, active triggers, compilation logs).

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#08080a`):** Deep obsidian void. The baseline canvas for all top-level layouts.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0d0f12`):** Sunken editorial panels, sidebars, and structural wells.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#16181d`):** Interactive planes, hover triggers, active telemetry bars.
- **Hairline Stroke (`#242831` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Used for all structural grid boundaries, metric fences, and input borders.
- **Primary Accent (`#10b981`):** Muted emerald. Reserved for primary calls-to-action, success markers, and active status pings.
- **Secondary Accent (`#2dd4bf`):** Electric mint / cyan highlight. Used sparingly for interactive focus rings, code tags, and micro-diff indicators.
- **Text Primary (`#f8fafc`):** Pristine stark white for maximum typographic impact against black backdrops.
- **Text Secondary (`#94a3b8`):** Steel mist for sub-headlines, reading paragraphs, and index markers.
- **Text Muted (`#475569`):** Graphite mute for timestamps, grid coordinates, and metadata labels.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy is governed by deliberate stylistic tension: the expressive geometric tension of **Space Grotesk**, the hyper-legible neutrality of **Inter**, and the systematic precision of **JetBrains Mono**.

### Typographic Principles
- **Display & Headlines:** Space Grotesk is set with tight negative letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) and compact line heights to create solid, monolithic visual blocks.
- **Body Text:** Inter brings crisp legibility to project narratives, case studies, and engineering breakdowns, spaced generously for relaxed editorial reading.
- **Telemetry & Labels:** JetBrains Mono powers all breadcrumbs, indexing numbers (e.g., `01 / 08`), technical stack tags, and live clock telemetry. Set with wide letter spacing (`0.08em`) and uppercase styling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column asymmetric Swiss grid with generous outer margins and disciplined structural hairpins. Rather than centering components within isolated cards, content aligns to rigorous horizontal and vertical axes spanning edge-to-edge.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Fluid single-column flow with full-bleed horizontal divider rules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 2rem`. Asymmetric splits (e.g., 3-col project metadata, 5-col narrative body).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, `margin-desktop: 4rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2.5rem`. Maximum content width bounded at `1600px` for ultra-wide displays.

### Composition Rules
- **Full-Bleed Hairlines:** Section borders stretch across the full viewport width, framing projects like blueprint drawings.
- **Negative Space Breathing Room:** Utilize `space-2xl` for vertical rhythm between case studies and interactive demonstrations.
- **Asymmetry:** Feature headlines align left to Column 1, while telemetry indexes dock to Column 12, maximizing visual tension across the screen.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through stark tonal layering and translucent light diffusion rather than conventional multi-layered shadows.

### Elevation Stratum
- **Level 0 (Void):** Deepest black `#08080a`. Unchanging canvas background.
- **Level 1 (Sub-surface):** Rich obsidian `#0d0f12`. Delimited exclusively by a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Flight Layer):** Backdrop blur planes (`rgba(13, 15, 18, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Used for sticky navigation rails, floating terminal panels, and docked toolbars.

### Ambient Bioluminescence
- Traditional black drop shadows are forbidden.
- Interactive focus states and high-priority indicators emit a directional emerald bloom:
  `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.18)`.
- Kinetic highlights utilize a radial gradient aura tracking the cursor over 1px hairline borders (`background: radial-gradient(400px circle at var(--x) var(--y), rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.12), transparent 80%)`).

## Shapes

The design system is strictly sharp-edged (`roundedness: 0`). 

### Geometry & Form
- **Zero Radius:** All elements—buttons, visual modules, dialog panes, image frames, and input controls—bear an absolute `0px` border radius. 
- **Architectural Edge:** Unbroken square corners communicate technical rigor, structural durability, and minimal brutalist elegance.
- **Exceptions:** Circular geometry is reserved strictly for operational status dots (e.g., green pulsing availability indicator) and focal cursor halos.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Stark obsidian background (`#08080a`), 1px solid emerald stroke (`#10b981`), crisp white monospaced label (`JetBrains Mono`, 12px, tracking `0.08em`, uppercase). On hover, transitions instantly into solid emerald background (`#10b981`) with ink-black text (`#08080a`) and an ambient emerald aura.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px solid hairline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text in steel mist (`#94a3b8`). On hover, border shifts to stark white (`#f8fafc`) with text `#f8fafc`.
- **Corner Accent Detail:** High-priority CTAs feature microscopic square notches or hairline crosshairs (`+`) at the top-right and bottom-left bounds.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Structure:** No floating white-box cards. Work items are laid out as border-delineated editorial cells.
- **Borders:** Separated by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over a showcase row or tile triggers a smooth luminance shift from `#08080a` to `#0f1217`, with project title text in Space Grotesk translating 8px to the right and an accent glyph arrow sliding diagonally upward (`↗`).

### Input Fields & Terminal Controls
- **Form Fields:** Flat background (`#0d0f12`), single bottom-only hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Typography:** Value input in `Inter` 15px; floating label in `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase (`#475569`).
- **Focus State:** Hairline border turns into solid `#2dd4bf` with a subtle 2px vertical caret in electric cyan.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Display:** Monospaced (`JetBrains Mono`), 11px uppercase, padded `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally.
- **Styling:** Inset black background with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Live State Variant:** Contains a leading 6px pulsing emerald dot (`#10b981`), signifying active server uptime, deployment success, or availability for freelance/hire.

### Data Lists & Index Rows
- **Layout:** Monolithic 2-column or 4-column horizontal rules. Left side contains chronological index (`01`, `02`, `03`) in muted monospace; center hosts the headline and role description; right side details tech stack tags and release year.
- **Divider:** Every row is partitioned by a `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` border.
